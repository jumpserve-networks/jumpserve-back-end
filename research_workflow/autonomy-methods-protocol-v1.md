# Autonomous method completion: development protocol v1

Frozen before implementation on 2026-10-28. Trigger: production assessment
`b5a2a984-0ce7-44c9-a7a5-f12a87559c7f` stopped after 69 recorded attempts,
with two source-arithmetic runs, two inconclusive and seven untested tasks.
Those records are development evidence, not held-out validation.

Questions: can a recoverable design gap reach the native builder; can complete
source arithmetic and prospective numerical comparisons reach an evidence-based
decision without prepopulated claim metrics; can a reversed operand order and
incomplete, invalid, altered or incorrectly shared evidence be detected?

Release-blocking development criteria:

1. Design/implementation blockers reach the builder when enabled. Equipment,
   authorization, provider and budget blockers cannot bypass existing admission.
2. Server-owned instructions require autonomous, justified prospective choices
   for unreported settings, frozen comparison criteria and explicit deviations.
   They forbid inventing source settings, source hashes or observations.
3. Numerical decisions use frozen rule/configuration identities and full recorded
   runs, verify source/spec/output hashes, units, counts and replications, and
   retain source values separately. Missing/invalid/ambiguous is never zero.
4. Source-arithmetic reviews receive explicit ordered expressions. Recomputing
   an erroneous frozen expression must not silently correct an original run.
5. Shared acquisitions retain claim-specific rules and configuration mappings;
   observations on one host are not extra independent replications. Rules use
   descriptive whole-run means, not unsupported confidence intervals.
6. Causal/generalization claims may collect diagnostic experiments, but a
   descriptive comparator cannot certify their broader interpretation.
7. Controller paths preserve every attempt, source and result, and original
   $100/seven-day limits. No forced verdict, limit reset or production mutation.

Checks: Python unittest development regressions, source-derived replay of the
retained public numerical evidence, full backend suite, and syntax checks. No
new empirical result or live-model accuracy is established by these checks.

Before production release: evaluate actual planning/review/assessment answers
under a separately frozen bounded live protocol, retain prompts/responses/usage
and failures, qualify exact runtime bytes, and explicitly resume selected tasks
under the original paper budget/deadline after reconciling existing identities.
No automatic deployment or resumption is authorized by this development protocol.
